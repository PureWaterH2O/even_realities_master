## Part A — Desk readable-transcript features (at the desk terminal)

**A1. Scrollback viewport** — *Trigger:* ask for long output (e.g. "read <a big file> and summarize each section").
*Expected:* transcript fills a fixed viewport (not infinite-append); scroll up/down works; a scroll-position
indicator shows; host-terminal scrollback isn't fighting it; streaming content stays pinned while older content
is reachable.

and readable.

not raw `#`/`*`.
